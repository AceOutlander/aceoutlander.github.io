Tin Hats privacy policy

The short version

  • No account with us.The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • No servers of ours.We run no game servers and no analytics of our own. We never receive your save, your purchases or anything about your device.
  • Nothing is sold.We do not sell or rent data about players, and we have none to sell.
  • Google does three jobs in the Android app.Payments (Google Play), the optional cloud save (Google Play Games) and the optional ads (Google AdMob). Each is described below.
  • Ads only when you ask.The game never shows an ad by itself. One plays only when you press "Watch" in the Shop, and nothing is sent to Google's ad service before that.
  • You can delete everything.Uninstall the app or clear its data, and delete the saved game from your Google account. The steps are below.

What the game keeps on your device

Tin Hats is played offline. Everything it needs is inside the app, and it works in airplane mode. It keeps these on your device only:

  • Your save: missions won, medals, upgrades, call-in charges and your settings.
  • Purchase records (Android app): for each purchase, which item it was, Google Play's order number, the date and the medals given. They make sure a purchase is given once and never lost. They do not hold your name or any payment details.
  • Ad records (Android app): how many reward ads you watched today, so the daily limit can be kept, and your answer to Google's ad privacy question if you were asked one.

The browser version on this site keeps only the save, in your browser's storage for this site. It has no purchases, no ads and no cloud save.

Android's own backup

If backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the save and the purchase records to your own Google account, so they come back on a new phone. That is a feature of Android run by Google; you can switch it off in the phone's settings under Google, Backup.

Cloud save (optional, Android app)

The game does not ask you to sign in. If you choose "Sign in" under Cloud save in Settings, the app uses Google Play Games to keep one saved game in your own Google account. It holds your save, the purchase records described above, a random number that tells your devices apart, and the time it was written. It holds no name, no email address and no payment details.

The app shows your Play Games player name on the Settings screen so you can see which account is signed in. The name is not stored by the game and is not sent anywhere.

The saved game lives in your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. We cannot see it, read it or delete it for you.

Purchases (Android app)

Medal packs and the Cavalier are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment: the game never sees your card, your billing address or your Google account's email address. Google tells the app only that a purchase was made, which item it was, its order number and its date, and the app checks that message is genuine on your device.

As the seller of record, we can see orders in the Google Play Console (order number, item, price, country and state), as every developer on Google Play can. We use that only to answer questions about a purchase and to give refunds. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments privacy notice.

Ads (optional, Android app)

The only ads in Tin Hats are reward ads that you choose to watch: press "Watch" in the Shop and you get a few medals for watching one, a few times a day. There are no banners, no ads between missions and no ads that start by themselves. If you never press "Watch", the ad service is never started and nothing is sent to it.

The ads come from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). When you ask for an ad, Google's software in the app collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and similar device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which Google may estimate your general location (such as your city or region);
  • which ads you were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data of the ad software.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to prevent fraud. Depending on your choices it may also use it to show ads based on your interests. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We do not receive this information about you; AdMob shows us only totals, such as how many ads were watched.

Your choices

  • Do not watch ads. They are never required. Everything in the game can be played without them.
  • The consent question. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, Google's form asks for your choice before the first ad. You can change it at any time in the game's Settings under "Ad privacy choices" (the button appears once you have been asked).
  • Your phone's ad settings. In Android's settings under Privacy, Ads (or Google, Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID, which stops ads based on that ID in every app.
  • Your Google account. My Ad Center lets you switch personalised ads off.

The ads are limited to content rated for a general audience with parental guidance (Google's "PG" level).

Children

Tin Hats is a war game for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the ads or the cloud save, the steps in the next section remove everything the game keeps; you can also write to us.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is kept on servers of ours, you delete your data yourself, and it takes effect at once:

  • On the device: uninstall the app, or open Android's settings, Apps, Tin Hats, Storage, and choose "Clear storage". In the game, Settings, "Reset progress" wipes the save (medals you paid for are given back). In the browser version, clear this site's data in your browser.
  • The cloud save: open the Google Play Games app, Settings, "Delete Play Games account & data", and delete the data for Tin Hats; or use the Data & privacy page of your Google account.
  • Android's backup: switch backup off or delete the backup in the phone's settings under Google, Backup.
  • Ad information held by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings, and use My Ad Center and My Activity in your Google account.
  • Purchase history: Google keeps your order history in your Google Play account as part of its payment records. We cannot delete it.

If you delete the save and the cloud save, medals you have bought are gone with them. The Cavalier stays with your Google Play account and comes back when you install the game again.

Security, storage and changes

Everything the app sends goes to Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The save and the records are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. They are kept until you delete them as described above.

If the game changes in a way that changes this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The Android app's permissions are the ones Google's payment, saved game and ad software need: internet access, the network state, billing, the advertising ID and Android's ad services.
